Caliper: Darkroom, seventh pass

The room is dark and the device is the only lit thing in it. There is no accent hue: the product is the only colour on screen, and selection, focus and the live knob use ink. Takes live on the canvas as chains: the real files, then each chain as a before-and-after pair. You mark a take with lettered pins and regions (docs/plans/take-markup.md), and one Send makes the next take of every marked chain. In light mode the room becomes a lightbox. Each state below is the same page with ?state=; open it on this phone to see it at real size in your system's colour scheme.
