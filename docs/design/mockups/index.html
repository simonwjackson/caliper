Caliper: Darkroom, second pass

The room is dark and the device is the only lit thing in it. There is no accent hue: the product is the only colour on screen, and selection, focus and the live knob use ink. Takes live on the canvas beside the real files, and you comment on a take by dropping numbered pins on it. In light mode the room becomes a lightbox. Each state below is the same page with ?state=; open it on this phone to see it at real size in your system's colour scheme.
